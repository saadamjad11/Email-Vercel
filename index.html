06FAQ

Common questions

What services do you offer? +

Graphic design, social media creatives, digital marketing support and brand visuals — covering everything from billboards and brochures to carousels, Stories and platform‑specific social content.

What's your design process? +

It starts with your brief and brand references. I'll share initial directions, refine based on your feedback, and deliver final files once you're happy with the result.

How long does a project take? +

It depends on scope — a single social post is quicker than a full campaign set. Send your brief through the form below or on WhatsApp and I'll give you a realistic timeline.

Do you work with agencies and ongoing retainers? +

Yes — I have hands‑on experience supporting marketing teams and agencies with ongoing content production, working from campaign calendars and briefs on a recurring basis.

What file formats do you deliver? +

Export formats appropriate to where the design is going — platform‑ready images for social, print‑ready files for billboards and brochures, and source files on request.

Can you design within an existing brand guide? +

Yes — translating a brief or existing brand guide into consistent, on‑brand visuals across every asset is a core part of how I work.
